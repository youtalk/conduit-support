    Drive a mobile robot

    Conduit sends sensor_msgs/Joy at 50 Hz while the drive view is open and the robot link is up, reliable with KEEP_LAST 1, on the profile's Joy output topic (default /joy). The layout is that of the ROS 2 joy package's game_controller_node, so teleop_twist_joy reads it as it reads a gamepad.

    The drive view

    iPhone

    The drive view follows how you hold the phone: landscape for two hands, upright for one. Turn the phone to switch; with the system's rotation lock on, it stays upright. A turn lets go of every input, so press again to drive.

    • Landscape: status chips at the top left, the gear and close at the top right. At the bottom, the joystick is under one thumb (the left one unless you change it in the gear panel), the mini map is in the other corner, and the status banner is between them.
    • Upright: the same top row, the status banner above the bottom row, the joystick in the bottom corner of the hand that holds the phone (the right hand unless you change it), and the mini map in the other corner.

    The chips show the battery, the speed from the odometry, the camera's latency and frame rate (the frame rate only when the robot's and the phone's clocks disagree; "No video" before the first frame; red while the camera is STALE) and the diagnostics (tap it for the full list). The battery, speed and diagnostics chips appear once they have a value. Pinch the camera to scale it between the whole image and filling the screen, and drag to pan it; Conduit keeps the zoom for each orientation. The mini map shows when the profile has a Map or LaserScan topic; tap it for the full-screen map.

    iPad and Mac

    The camera fills two thirds of the width, next to a column of Map, Status and Diagnostics tiles. Drag the divider between them to resize (the camera keeps at least 40 % of the width); double-tap it to restore the default. Tap a tile to open it in a sheet. Pinch and drag the camera to zoom it within its tile. With a hardware keyboard connected, the hint "Space + arrows or WASD to drive" shows above the banner. In a narrow iPad window the drive view uses the iPhone's landscape layout.

    Gear panel

    • Max speed: 25%, 50%, 75% or 100% of the stick's full travel, for the forward speed and the turn rate alike. It applies to the on-screen joystick, the keyboard and a game controller's left stick. Every drive view opens at 50%. Changing it while you drive releases every input until you press again, so the robot never jumps to the new speed.
    • Stick (iPhone, landscape): Left thumb or Right thumb for the joystick.
    • Hand (iPhone, upright): Left hand or Right hand, the hand that holds the phone.

    Conduit keeps the Stick and Hand choices per robot on this device. The robot keeps driving while the gear panel is open.

    Map and navigation goals

    The full-screen map (a sheet on iPad and Mac) pinch-zooms between 0.5× and 20×, pans with a drag, and resets with a double tap. Nothing drives while the map, Status or Diagnostics is open; the banner says "Paused while a panel is open", and after you close it every input needs a fresh press.

    To send a navigation goal, set the profile's Goal pose topic (/goal_pose for Nav2). The profile also needs a Map topic. Then, on the full-screen map:

    1. Tap Set goal.
    2. Touch the goal and drag toward the heading the robot should face. A tap without a drag points the goal away from the robot.

    Conduit sends one geometry_msgs/PoseStamped in the profile's Map frame, with a zero stamp (the latest transform), like RViz's 2D Goal Pose tool, and then returns to panning. It waits up to 2 s for a subscriber and then sends anyway. The map says "Goal sent on <topic>", or "Goal sent, but nothing subscribes to <topic>" when no subscriber matched. The goal stays on the map until you close the drive view.

    Conduit cannot cancel a goal. Releasing the joystick, closing the map or closing the drive view does not stop the robot driving to it.

    Inputs

    input Joy field role
    stick up / down axes[1] (up +) linear.x
    stick left / right axes[0] (left +) angular.z
    touching the stick, Space, or the left shoulder buttons[9] deadman (enable_button)
    right shoulder buttons[10] not used: turbo is off, and Conduit masks it unless the deadman is held

    See Joy layout for every axis and button.

    • On-screen joystick: the touch input in every case, also with VoiceOver and with a game controller connected. Touch it to drive and lift your finger to stop.
    • Game controllers (MFi and other GameController devices): hold LB / L1 and use the left stick. The "Game controller" chip next to the joystick shows that one is connected; you can use either.
    • Hardware keyboard (Mac, or an iPad with a keyboard): hold Space and use the arrows or WASD. The keys drive at the max speed.
    • VoiceOver: the joystick takes direct touch. It is labelled "Drive joystick" with the hint "Drag to drive; lift your finger to stop", and VoiceOver announces "Driving" and "Stopped".
    • After a lost link, the app going to the background, a closed panel, a turn of the phone or a max speed change, nothing drives until you let go of every input and press again; while you still hold one, the banner says "Release and touch again to drive". While the link is down it says "Disconnected", and while Conduit is inactive "Paused while Conduit is inactive".
    • Without Conduit Plus, a chip over the camera shows the week's free-time notices; tap it to dismiss it. When the free time runs out, the robot stops and the drive view stays behind the Conduit Plus paywall until you close it.
    • The Game Controller sensor publishes <namespace>/joy (/conduit/joy by default); the drive view publishes the profile's Joy output topic (/joy by default). Run conduit_teleop on one of them, not both. If the profile's Joy output is the sensor's topic and the sensor is enabled, Conduit asks before it opens the drive view and pauses the sensor while the drive view is open.

    Host setup

    Install conduit_teleop as described in Teleoperation → Host setup and run it with its default joy_topic (/joy) as described in Teleoperation → Drive view.

    conduit_teleop runs teleop_twist_joy with Conduit's parameter file and a watchdog. With the shipped file, full stick is 0.5 m/s and 1.0 rad/s, so the default max speed of 50% tops out at 0.25 m/s and 0.5 rad/s. The watchdog forwards teleop_twist_joy's Twist to cmd_vel while the newest Joy is younger than 0.5 s. When the Joy goes stale it publishes zero twists at 10 Hz for 1 s and then stays silent until Joy resumes (it is also silent before the first Joy), so it does not fight Nav2 or another cmd_vel publisher.

    Kachaka

    The kachaka-api ROS 2 bridge takes velocity on /kachaka/manual_control/cmd_vel once manual control is enabled. On the host, run conduit_teleop's Kachaka launch file, which sets both up and drives at Kachaka's own speeds (Teleoperation → Kachaka). It starts conduit_teleop itself, so use it instead of conduit_teleop.launch.py, not together with it:

    ros2 launch conduit_teleop kachaka.launch.py

    Profile: Camera /kachaka/front_camera/image_raw/compressed, Map /kachaka/mapping/map, Map frame map, Base frame base_footprint, Goal pose /kachaka/goal_pose, the LaserScan, Odometry and Battery topics the bridge lists, Joy output /joy.
