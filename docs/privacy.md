# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 9, 2026**

Conduit is a developer tool that publishes sensor data from iPhone, iPad and Mac to ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2) networks. It is built on the open-source [swift-ros2](https://github.com/swift-ros2/swift-ros2) library, which speaks the ROS 2 wire formats directly over Zenoh or DDS. There is no Conduit-operated server in between. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and your choices.

**Developer**: Yutaka Kondo (youtalk) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/youtalk) · [Website](https://youtalk.jp)

Parts marked **Conduit 3.0** describe features that arrive with Conduit 3.0. They do not apply to earlier versions.

## Sensor data

Conduit reads a sensor **only after you turn that sensor on**. All sensors are off by default. Depending on what you turn on, this can include:

- **Motion**: accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer (IMU, Magnetic Field)
- **Location**: GPS position
- **Camera**: images from the device cameras
- **Depth and pose**: LiDAR depth and the device's position and orientation from ARKit (on supported devices)
- **Audio**: microphone samples
- **Environment**: air pressure (Barometer) and a brightness estimate from the camera (Illuminance)
- **Proximity**: the near/far reading of the proximity sensor
- **Device state**: battery level and thermal state
- **Game controller input**: buttons and sticks of a connected controller

Where it goes:

- **Live publishing**: Conduit sends sensor data **only to the Zenoh router or DDS peers you configure**, usually on your own network. It is not sent to us or to any third party.
- **Recordings**: when you record, Conduit writes the same sensor messages to an `.mcap` file on the device. Recordings stay on the device unless you share them (for example through the share sheet, Files or AirDrop). Conduit never uploads them.
- **Background publishing** (Conduit 3.0): if you turn on Background Publishing, Conduit keeps publishing while it is in the background as long as the GPS or Microphone sensor is on. Camera and LiDAR stop while Conduit is in the background. iOS shows that Conduit is using your location or microphone, and on iPhone a Live Activity on the Lock Screen shows the running session.
- **Location** access is "While Using the App" only. Conduit reads your location only while the GPS sensor is on, including in the background when Background Publishing is on.

## Robots (Conduit 3.0)

When you open a robot in the Robots tab, Conduit connects through the Zenoh router or DDS peers you configure, as it does for publishing, and:

- **receives** the topics you pick for that robot, such as camera images, a map, laser scans, odometry, battery state and diagnostics, and shows them on screen. Conduit does not record or store what it receives, and does not send it anywhere else;
- **sends** your commands to the robot: controller input (`sensor_msgs/Joy`), arm motion commands (velocity, poses and gripper) and navigation goals.

Arm teleoperation follows the device's motion from ARKit (Device Pose), which uses the camera. Robot profiles (a robot's name, topics and settings) are stored on the device.

## Connecting with a QR code

When you scan a pairing QR code from the Conduit pairing script ([Connect with a QR code](pair.md)):

- The connection settings are in the part of the link after `#`. If the link opens in a browser, the browser does not send that part to the web server; the page only passes it to Conduit.
- After Conduit applies the settings, it sends one line of JSON over TCP to the pairing script on your computer, at the address in the QR code. The line contains:
    - the one-time token from the QR code,
    - the device's name (as set in iOS or iPadOS Settings),
    - the Conduit version,
    - the ROS 2 node name and namespace set in Conduit.

- The script uses this to confirm that the device checked in. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Usage statistics

Conduit uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous usage statistics:

- **Device information**: device model, OS version
- **App usage**: for example which sensors are turned on or off, connection starts, stops and errors, and settings changes
- **Anonymous identifiers**: an app instance identifier generated by Firebase

Conduit 3.0 also sends:

- **Build type**: whether the app came from the App Store, TestFlight or a development build
- **Connection results**: whether a connection attempt succeeded, the transport (Zenoh or DDS), the ROS 2 distribution and, for a failure, an error category
- **Robots**: that a robot or its drive view was opened, and whether the robot is a mobile robot or an arm
- **Conduit Plus**: your plan (Free, Plus or Founder); when the Conduit Plus screen appears and where it was opened from; when the weekly free time runs out and which feature was in use; and purchases: the product, its price and currency

Conduit does not send sensor values, recordings, anything your robots send or receive (topics, images, maps and so on), how much free time you have used, or the addresses and names you enter in Settings. Firebase may derive a coarse location, such as the country, from the network connection. We use these statistics only to find bugs and improve the app. They are processed by Google under the [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

To turn them off, open Settings in Conduit → **About & Help** → **Privacy** and turn off **Send Usage Statistics**. This also stops purchases from being counted.

## Data stored on the device

- **Settings** (connection, sensors, rates and so on) and **robot profiles** (Conduit 3.0) are stored on the device.
- **Recordings** stay on the device until you delete them in Conduit or in Files, or delete the app. In Conduit 3.0 they are kept in Conduit's folder in the Files app.
- **Conduit Plus** (Conduit 3.0): the free time used this week, and whether you are a Founder, are kept in the Keychain on the device. They are never sent anywhere. iOS may keep Keychain items after you delete the app.
- Earlier versions of Conduit stored a random 16-byte ROS 2 publisher ID in the Keychain. Current versions do not use it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Local Network | Connect to the Zenoh router or DDS peers on your network, and to the pairing script | Yes, to publish or to open a robot |
| Camera | Camera images; also used by LiDAR, Device Pose (and with it arm teleoperation) and Illuminance | Optional |
| Microphone | Audio samples | Optional |
| Location (While Using the App) | GPS position, also in the background when Background Publishing is on (Conduit 3.0) | Optional |
| Motion & Fitness | IMU and magnetometer | Optional |
| Notifications (Conduit 3.0) | While Conduit publishes in the background: tell you that the weekly free time is running out or used up, or that the connection was lost | Optional |

Conduit asks for a permission when you first use a feature that needs it. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, and notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications.

## Purchases

- **Conduit 3.0**: every feature can be used for some free time each week. Conduit Plus, a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase, removes the limit. If you bought any of the one-time features of earlier versions, you have Conduit Plus at no charge (Founder).
- **Earlier versions**: some features are one-time In-App Purchases: Back Cameras (Wide, Ultra Wide and Telephoto; the front camera is free), LiDAR, Game Controller, Background Mode and Recording Export.

Apple handles payment, receipts, refunds and subscriptions. Conduit learns from the App Store which products you own and whether a subscription is active; it does not see or store payment information. In Conduit 3.0, purchases are also counted in the usage statistics (see above). See Apple's [App Store & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/) page.

## Your choices

- **Sensors**: turn any sensor on or off at any time.
- **Usage statistics**: turn off **Send Usage Statistics** in Conduit's Settings → About & Help.
- **Recordings**: delete them in Conduit at any time.
- **Subscriptions** (Conduit 3.0): manage or cancel Conduit Plus in your Apple Account's subscription settings.
- **All app data**: delete the app to remove its settings, robot profiles and recordings.

## Data retention

- **Live sensor data**: not kept by the app; it is sent as it is captured.
- **What a robot sends** (Conduit 3.0): not kept; it is shown on screen only.
- **Recordings**: kept on the device until you delete them or delete the app.
- **Settings and robot profiles**: kept until you delete the app.
- **Weekly free time and Founder status** (Conduit 3.0): kept in the Keychain on the device; the free time starts again every week.
- **Usage statistics**: kept by Firebase under [its retention policy](https://support.google.com/firebase/answer/7029846).
