# Conduit Plus

Every feature of Conduit is free for **5 hours of use every week**, including features added
later. Conduit Plus removes the limit. No feature is for Conduit Plus only: the weekly time is the
only difference.

## What counts as use

Time counts while Conduit does at least one of these, and only once when it does several at the
same time:

- sends a topic that a connected subscriber receives (publishing with nobody subscribed does not
  count);
- records an MCAP file, with or without publishing;
- has a robot's drive view or arm view open with the robot connected.

Time is counted by the second. On iPhone and iPad, publishing and recording keep counting in the
background, and a drive view counts only while Conduit is on screen. On a Mac, everything counts
in the background too.

The week starts on Monday at 00:00 in the device's time zone. Unused time does not carry over to
the next week. The time used is kept on the device.

**Settings** → **Conduit Plus** shows the time left this week and when it refills. While you are
on the free plan, the Start button shows the time left too ("3h 12m left this week").

## When the time runs out

- With 30 minutes and with 5 minutes left, Conduit warns once: "30 minutes of free time left this
  week" and "5 minutes left — the session stops when this week's free time is used up". While
  Conduit runs in the background, the warnings come as notifications, if you allow them.
- When the time is used up, Conduit cancels a robot's navigation goal and sends its stop, closes
  the recording (the file stays complete and indexed), stops publishing and shows Conduit Plus.
- While you hold a robot's drive control (the joystick, a game controller's deadman button or
  HOLD), or a robot is driving to a navigation goal, Conduit waits until you let go or the goal
  ends, for at most 5 more minutes ("Free time is up. The robot is finishing its goal (up to 5
  min).").
- Until the next Monday, Start opens Conduit Plus instead ("No free time left this week").

## Plans

| | Free | Monthly | Yearly | Lifetime | Founder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Use** | 5 hours a week | No limit | No limit | No limit | No limit |
| **Features** | Every feature | Every feature | Every feature | Every feature | Every feature |
| **Payment** | None | Every month | Every year | Once | None |
| **Renewal** | — | Automatic until you cancel | Automatic until you cancel | — | — |
| **Settings shows** | Free | Conduit Plus | Conduit Plus | Conduit Plus · Lifetime | Conduit Plus · Founder |
| **How to get it** | Every new user | Upgrade | Upgrade | Upgrade | A one-time feature of an earlier version ([Founder](#founder)) |

Conduit shows the prices in your currency. To buy, open **Settings** → **Conduit Plus** and tap
**Upgrade**, or choose a plan wherever Conduit shows Conduit Plus. While a subscription is active,
cancel it before you buy Lifetime.

## Founder

If you bought any of the one-time features of Conduit 2 (Back Cameras, LiDAR, Game Controller,
Background Mode or Recording Export) before December 21, 2026, you have Conduit Plus at no charge,
for good, including features added to Conduit Plus later:
**Settings** → **Conduit Plus** shows the plan as "Conduit Plus · Founder". Conduit finds the
purchase on the App Store by itself. On a new device, tap **Restore Purchases** if it does not.

## Manage your plan

- **Restore Purchases** (in **Settings** → **Conduit Plus**, and on the Conduit Plus screen)
  brings back the purchases of your Apple Account.
- **Manage Subscription** (in **Settings** → **Conduit Plus**, while a subscription is active)
  opens your Apple Account's subscriptions, where you change or cancel it (on a Mac, in the
  browser).
- A subscription keeps Conduit Plus during Apple's billing grace period. When Conduit cannot reach
  the App Store (offline in the field, for example), a subscription keeps working for up to 7
  days after its renewal date.
- A refunded purchase ends Conduit Plus. A session that is running finds out within 24 hours.

Apple handles payment, receipts, refunds and subscriptions. What Conduit keeps on the device for
Conduit Plus, and what it sends, is in the [Privacy policy](privacy.md).
